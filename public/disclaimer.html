What a verdict means

GO, CAUTION and STOP summarize automated checks listed with reason codes in each response. GO means only that every check StockFi ran passed at the time of the call. It does not mean a trade is safe, suitable or advisable, and it says nothing about price direction. Data can be late, missing or wrong, and a check can pass while a risk it does not measure is present.
